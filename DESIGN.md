## Architectural Foundations

Every outreach hero redesign demo serves as high-converting tangible proof of Miskat Hossain's web design caliber. Rather than applying a single template, designs are organized into five distinct **Brand Archetypes**, each with its own layout rhythm, color theory, typography, and functional interactive widget.

---

## The 5 Brand Archetypes

### 1. Clinical Medicine & Integrative Health
- **Target Profiles**: Medical clinics, functional neurology, metabolic health, trauma therapy, somatic healing.
- **Visual Canvas**: Pure sterile or warm clinical white (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`). Soft sage, slate teal, or clinical navy accents derived from the real practice logo.
- **Typography**: Clean, high-legibility geometric sans (Plus Jakarta Sans, Inter) paired with calm editorial serifs where appropriate.
- **Hero Architecture**: Split layout featuring clinic accreditation, licensed practitioner credentials, verified patient outcomes, and a **Live Clinical Intake / Symptom Triage Tool** or **Telehealth Booking Pathway**.
- **Imagery**: Real clinic interior photography, practitioner portrait in clinical or warm professional attire, and recognized medical certifications.

### 2. High-Ticket M&A Advisory & Executive Advisory
- **Target Profiles**: Private equity advisory, corporate turnaround, procurement consulting, founder exit planning.
- **Visual Canvas**: Architectural white and deep charcoal/navy (`#0A192F`, `#0F172A`, `#1E293B`). High-contrast, austere, institutional.
- **Typography**: Prestigious serif headlines (Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond) paired with ultra-clean grotesque sans body text.
- **Hero Architecture**: Asymmetric executive layout featuring lifetime transaction volume, discrete confidential advisory application, and an interactive **Exit Readiness Scorecard** or **Deal Sourcing Matrix**.
- **Imagery**: High-resolution founder portrait, confidential client enterprise credentials, and published advisory whitepapers.

### 3. Keynote Speaker, Bestselling Author & Performing Artist
- **Target Profiles**: TEDx speakers, award-winning authors, international keynote speakers, master coaches.
- **Visual Canvas**: Cinematic editorial or theatrical canvas (warm cream `#FAF8F5`, deep sapphire, or rich plum). High-fashion editorial spacing.
- **Typography**: Striking editorial typography with tight letterspacing and fluid hierarchy.
- **Hero Architecture**: Media marquee (TEDx, Forbes, Oprah, BBC), high-contrast keynote booking pathway, hardcover book showcase with chapter preview or instant assessment.
- **Imagery**: Real stage photography, high-end studio portrait with authentic personality, and published book jacket mockups.

### 4. Non-Profit, Humanitarian & Social Impact
- **Target Profiles**: Community shelters, veteran recovery, social enterprises, animal advocacy, humanitarian foundations.
- **Visual Canvas**: Warm, human-centered canvas (`#FFFFFF`, `#FDFBF7`) paired with their trademark cause colors (e.g. Viola Orchid `#BA22D2`, botanical green `#15803D`).
- **Typography**: Humanist sans with warm open letterforms.
- **Hero Architecture**: Transparent 501(c)(3) accreditation, GuideStar/CharityNavigator proof, 24/7 crisis ribbon, and an **Interactive Tiered Giving / Impact Sponsorship Calculator**.
- **Imagery**: Real community impact photography (shelter, community programs, recovery ranch), founder community roots, and transparent impact statistics.

### 5. High-Margin Commercial & Franchise Route Operations
- **Target Profiles**: Commercial route businesses, franchise systems, recurring-service operations.
- **Visual Canvas**: High-trust commercial navy, slate blue, and industrial gold/emerald on crisp white.
- **Typography**: Bold, confident sans (Archivo, Plus Jakarta Sans) conveying operational strength.
- **Hero Architecture**: Route density metrics, Item 19 financial disclosure transparency ($0 rent, weekly recurring subscriptions, turnkey fleet), and an interactive **Territory Exclusivity / Zip Code Checker**.
- **Imagery**: Real wrapped fleet vehicles, technician in uniform in actual residential/commercial settings, and Inc. 5000 / SBA medallions.

## Impeccable Craft Floor & Banned Habits

1. **NO Gradient Text on Headings**: Emphasis must come from typography scale, weight, or a solid brand accent color. Gradient clipping (`bg-clip-text text-transparent`) is strictly prohibited.
2. **NO Decorative Blur Glow Halos**: No floating colored orbs with `filter blur-3xl` placed behind cards. Depth is created via subtle borders (`border-slate-200`) and refined directional drop shadows.
3. **NO Cookie-Cutter Metric Docks**: Do not place an identical 4-box metric row under the CTAs on every page. Numbers must feel organic to the industry model.
4. **NO Stock Sunglasses Animals or Memes**: Real operational assets only. If real photos are unavailable, use clean typographic hierarchy and authentic brand logos.
5. **1440×900 Viewport Fit**: All essential value props, CTAs, interactive widgets, and proof elements must be comfortably visible within a standard 1440×900 desktop viewport without scrolling or clipping against bottom toolbars.
6. **Themed Browser Surfaces**: Every page must theme browser selection (`::selection`) and focus rings to match the brand palette.
