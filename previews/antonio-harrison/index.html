Designer notes

Why This Converts

Before: The current hero opens with a strong line ("The science of behavior, put into motion") but splits attention across four roles and five paths, so event organizers have to hunt for the booking route.

  • Outcome over identityThe headline sells what organizers buy (habits that stick for their people) and keeps his "science in motion" voice in the subhead.

  • Verified proof above the foldHis own numbers (30+ international keynotes, 15+ athletes mentored, Ph.D.) now sit beside the CTA instead of further down the page.

  • One primary route, four secondaryBooking leads; YouTube, CEUs, books and apparel move into a quiet "five ways in" row so they support the sale instead of competing with it.
