Why This Converts

Before: The Squarespace hero's H1 is just the word "XANDRA" (repeated four times as headings), so visitors learn what the firm does only after scrolling to "Marketing, coaching, and consulting".

  • Say what you do in the first lineThe headline states the unique offer (a marketing partner who also coaches you to sell) and the pain it removes (three separate vendors).

  • Founder-led trustAlexandra's portrait and name move into the hero; people buy PR and coaching from a person, not a wordmark.

  • Luxury palette, higher contrastHer black-and-sand brand is kept but tightened: sand becomes the CTA colour so the discovery call is impossible to miss.
