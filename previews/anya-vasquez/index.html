Why This Converts

Before: The hero headline breaks awkwardly ("I am Energy coach Anya -"), the promise is buried in a long H2, and "Let's connect" is the only action with no clear offer behind it.

  • A finished, outcome-led sentenceThe H1 states who it is for (mompreneurs), what changes (money and love) and the contrast she already uses (no quick fixes).

  • A named, specific first offer"Book Your Destiny Blueprint Reading" replaces "Let's connect", giving visitors a concrete, high-intent next step.

  • Warm luxury, higher contrastHer teal is deepened and paired with soft gold on ivory, so the brand feels premium while CTAs stay highly legible.
