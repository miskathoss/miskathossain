Why This Converts

Before: The GoDaddy hero has a good line ("Access To Care In Minutes Not Weeks") but no CTA in the first screen; "Contact Us" sits in the nav and the webinar promo pushes the explanation down.

  • Employer-specific promiseThe headline keeps their strongest phrase and adds the buyer (your workforce) and the pain (clinic trips and waiting rooms).

  • Two CTAs that match buying stagesReady employers book a demo; earlier-stage HR leaders reserve a webinar seat, which they are already promoting.

  • Ecosystem in one glanceWorkplace, travel and at-home access sit right under the hero image, so the model is understood without scrolling.
