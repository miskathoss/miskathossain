## Platform

web

## Users
High-earning founders, clinic directors, executive coaches, keynote speakers, M&A advisors, and non-profit leaders who receive custom LinkedIn cold outreach from Miskat Hossain.

## Product Purpose
High-converting interactive hero redesign demos hosted at `https://miskathossain.net/preview/<slug>`. Each demo proves in under 5 seconds that Miskat took the time to audit their existing website, diagnose real conversion & technical bottlenecks, extract their authentic brand palette and visual assets, and engineer a studio-grade hero section that elevates their authority and drives inbound client bookings.

## Positioning
Zero template cloning or AI slop. Unlike generic agencies sending unsolicited Figma mockups or template dumps, every demo is an interactive, production-ready web experience tailored to their exact industry archetype with real operational assets, transparent unit economics / clinical triage, and zero superficial gradient text or dark-mode clichés.

## Operating Context
- Delivery Channel: Personalized LinkedIn InMail / connection note containing the direct demo preview link and a concise site audit hook.
- Viewport & Device: Optimized for 1440×900 desktop viewing (where executives review agency pitches) and fully responsive down to mobile.
- Viewer State: Skeptical, time-poor decision-makers who immediately dismiss generic agency pitches but respond to custom-built proof of work with their own name, brand colors, and real business mechanics.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Code-first HTML/Tailwind CSS with responsive design, Lucide icons, and zero layout shift.
- Viewport lock: Hero section must fit cleanly within a 1440×900 desktop viewport without clipping metrics or crowding the bottom preview/audit dock.
- Palette fidelity: Strict extraction and adherence to the lead's authentic brand colors (no defaulting to dark mode or generic purple/blue).
- Real operational assets: Official cropped logos, real clinic/team/founder photography, published book mockups, or verified press/industry badges. Zero placeholder or cartoon art.
- Interactive lead mechanics: Custom interactive widgets per archetype (e.g. territory availability checker, clinical intake triage, sponsorship calculator, assessment diagnostic).
- Impeccable Craft Floor: Strict ban on gradient H1 text, kicker/eyebrow labels above headings, generic metric docks, and decorative background blur orbs. Themed browser surfaces (selection, focus rings, scrollbars).

## Brand Commitments
- Miskat Hossain Web & 3D Design Studio: World-class craft, technical rigor, bespoke attention to detail, and demonstrable ROI.
- Client Brand Fidelity: The redesign must feel like the authoritative, million-dollar evolution of their existing business—not a random template.

## Product Principles
1. **Authenticity Over Novelty**: Always extract and honor the lead's true trademark colors, typography voice, and visual assets before designing.
2. **Archetype-Specific Architecture**: A medical clinic does not look like an M&A advisory firm; an author/speaker does not look like a route franchise. Each receives a bespoke layout.
3. **Interactive Value Over Static Text**: Every hero must give the visitor something to interact with (a diagnostic, calculator, selector, or territory map) to prove functionality.
4. **Zero AI Slop**: No gradient-clipped text on headings, no decorative blur halos, no arbitrary 4-metric docks, and no ungrounded dark-mode styling.
5. **1440×900 Viewport Mastery**: The primary value proposition, CTAs, interactive widget, and visual proof must sit with comfortable whitespace in a 1440×900 viewport without clipping.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG AA contrast (≥4.5:1 text contrast).
- Accessible form fields with explicit labels and focus rings.
- Semantic HTML tags (`<header>`, `<main>`, `<h1>`, `<nav>`).
