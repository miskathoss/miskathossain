About Meg

A nurse first.
An injector second.

Meg (Megan, in full) is a registered nurse who works full-time in an emergency department and runs Aesthetics by Meg alongside her clinical role.

She trained in advanced dermal fillers, then completed basic and advanced botulinum toxin training at the Aesthetics Lounge Academy and skin booster training at H7 Cosmetics.

Her approach is simple. Aesthetics isn’t about changing who you are. It’s small, considered enhancements that help you feel like the best version of yourself.
