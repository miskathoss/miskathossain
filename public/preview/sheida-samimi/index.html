Redesign preview for Sheida Samimi · by Miskat Hossain

Site audit: sellwithonepercent.ca

What's holding the current home page back, and what this redesign changes.

Critical

Two different brokerages on one page

The logo and contact details say HomeLife Classic Realty; the hero banner, browser title and Google description say Sutton Group-Admiral Realty (and Newmarket). Visitors can't tell who they'd be signing with, and Ontario advertising rules expect the brokerage name to be clear.

FixOne brokerage name everywhere: header, about, contact and footer.

Name misspelled in the hero banner

The banner image reads "SHEIDA SAMIIMI". It's the first thing visitors see, and it can't be corrected without re-exporting the image.

FixAll text is live HTML, spelled correctly and editable in seconds.

The 1% offer disappears on phones

At 375px wide the banner doesn't render. The first screen on mobile is an empty "Featured Listings" heading, so the main selling point never appears.

Fix"Sell for 1%. Keep more of your equity." is the H1 on every screen size, with Sheida's photo and the evaluation button on the first screen.

Major

Featured Listings renders empty

The section sits right under the hero on desktop and mobile with no listings in it, which reads as a broken site.

FixReplaced with a live savings calculator; listing searches move to the About section as direct links.

No H1, headline baked into an image

Search engines and screen readers can't read the offer. The title and meta description target Newmarket, not Mississauga, Oakville or Toronto.

FixReal H1, semantic sections, and a title and description written for her three markets.

Slow first load

In our test the page took about 19 seconds to finish loading, with 49 requests and about 1.1 MB (single run; results vary by connection).

FixOne lightweight page: no jQuery bundles, CAPTCHA images or translate widget on first load.

"1%*" with no terms in sight

The asterisk never leads anywhere on the home page, which invites doubt about the offer.

FixPlain-language terms beside the calculator, under the hero example and in the footer.

Menu of roughly 50 links

Seller guides, buyer guides, alerts and searches compete equally, so the seller path gets lost.

FixFive clear destinations, with selling first.

Minor

Accessibility gaps

9 images have no alt text, 14 tap targets are under 32px tall, and the evaluation inputs have no visible labels.

FixLabelled fields with inline errors, 44px targets, alt text, visible focus rings and reduced-motion support.

Floating agent card covers content

The vertical photo tab overlaps the page on desktop and covers the portrait on mobile.

FixSheida's portrait leads the hero instead.

Dated signals

The footer reads "©2008", and the portrait is low resolution for modern screens.

FixCurrent footer. A high-resolution portrait from Sheida would sharpen the hero further.

Audit run on the live home page at 1440×900 and 375×812. Calculator figures are illustrative.
