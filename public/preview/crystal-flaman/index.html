Why Crystal

She's done the hard things she talks about.

Crystal has run 273 km across the Utah desert carrying everything she needed on her back, finished eleven Ironman races, and cycled the width of Canada on a tandem with her twin sister. She's also rebuilt her life after chronic illness and shattered bones.

Every one of those miles turned into something bigger: over $1.4 million raised, and schools, a reading room and scholarships for kids who wouldn't otherwise be in a classroom. That's the shift she brings to your stage, from personal success to real significance.

Redesign preview for Crystal Flaman · by Miskat Hossain

Site audit: crystalflaman.com

What's holding the current WordPress site back with event planners, and what this redesign changes.

7.7sfull load time
19H1 headings
1plugin closed for security

What the site runs on

WordPress 6.7 · Divi 4.27 with a custom "crystal" child theme · WooCommerce + Stripe + Woo Advanced Discounts · Contact Form 7 · The SEO Framework · Monarch sharing · Testimonial Rotator · Mailchimp signup · GA Google Analytics plugin

Critical

A closed plugin still runs the reviews slider

The home page testimonials use Testimonial Rotator. WordPress.org closed that plugin on 25 February 2021 for a security issue, so it hasn't received an update since.

FixReviews become static Divi Testimonial modules, and the plugin is deactivated and deleted.

The home page still talks about 2020

"In the current global situation… these unprecedented times" sits high on the home and Speaking pages, and the footer reads © 2019–2020. A planner shortlisting speakers today reads that as a site, or a business, that has gone quiet.

FixCurrent copy in the Divi text modules and a current footer. Live online events become one format among several, not a pandemic notice.

The hero speaks to fans, not to the people who book

"Discover what you need to make your dreams a reality" is a self-help message; nothing on the first screen says what Crystal speaks about or how to book her. On a 375px phone the headline is squeezed beside the photo and runs off the right edge, and the first booking link is about 3,200px down the page.

FixThe H1 names the outcome for an organization, with "Check availability" in the header and hero at every screen size.

Her strongest proof is buried in the About page

11 Ironman finishes, a 273 km desert ultra run twice, $1.4M+ raised and the first tandem ride across Canada sit in one long paragraph two clicks deep.

FixA proof band directly under the hero, then a "Why Crystal" story with her race photos.

Major

Store code loads on every page

WooCommerce, Stripe and the discounts plugin load their scripts and styles on the home page too, alongside jQuery Migrate, Thickbox, Dashicons, Font Awesome and an old TweenMax animation library. In our test the home page made 86 requests, about 2.4 MB, with 47 scripts and 20 stylesheets, and took about 7.7 seconds to finish loading (single run; results vary by connection).

FixLoad store assets only on Store, cart and checkout, drop the unused libraries, and load the TEDx video only on play.

The SEO plugin is there, but not set up

The SEO Framework is installed, yet the home page has no meta description. Divi modules output 19 H1 headings (the hero is built twice, once for phones), and the share preview points to three small icons.

FixA description written for event planners in The SEO Framework, one H1 with the rest set to H2/H3 in Divi, and a proper 1200×630 share image.

The booking form is long and hard to read

The Contact Form 7 form has 11 fields with ALL-CAPS placeholder text and no visible labels (the labels disappear as people type), plus a typo: "HOW CAN CAN CRYSTAL WOW YOUR AUDIENCE?" Her free 30-minute discovery session is only mentioned on the Contact page.

FixThe same Contact Form 7 form, with real labels, fewer required fields, a talk picker and the discovery call offered up front.

Eight talks on one long page, with no way to compare

The Speaking page is a single scroll of eight near-identical blocks, each ending in the same button.

FixA talk picker (a Divi Tabs module): promise, who it's for, takeaways and formats side by side, with the chosen talk carried into the booking form.

One testimonial on the home page

She has at least eight strong, named reviews, but the home page shows one inside a nine-dot slider.

FixThree attributed reviews with their organizations, plus the prism quote placed next to the keepsakes it describes.

Sections that stay blank until they animate

Nine Divi elements use entrance animations, so several blue panels look empty to quick scrollers until the animation fires.

FixContent is always visible; motion is limited to the hero and two signature moments.

Minor

Accessibility gaps

18 of 19 images on the home page have no alt text, 27 tap targets on mobile are under 32px tall, and pinch-to-zoom is switched off.

FixAlt text in the Media Library, 44px targets, labelled form fields, zoom allowed, visible focus and reduced-motion support.

A shopping cart competes with "Book Crystal"

The header cart icon and Store link get the same weight as Speaking, so the main path to revenue is diluted.

FixBooking leads; the store and Inspiration Corner stay, but move to the footer.

The keepsakes go unmentioned

Prisms, rings and workbooks for audiences are a real differentiator, but they're buried in bullet lists under each talk.

FixA dedicated "What your audience takes home" section.

Stays in WordPress

No new platform needed

Every section of this redesign can be built in her existing WordPress and Divi setup: Tabs for the talks, Testimonial modules for reviews, the Video module with an image overlay for TEDx, and her current Contact Form 7 form. WooCommerce, Stripe, Mailchimp and her blog stay exactly as they are.

Audit run on the live site (home, About, Speaking, Contact and Rave Reviews pages) at 1440×900 and 375×812. The stack was read from the public page source; plugin status from WordPress.org. Copy, photos and reviews in this preview come from crystalflaman.com.
